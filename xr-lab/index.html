Digital Skills LabVR and AR learning prototype

How to use

  • Desktop: drag to look around, click to select.
  • Phone: move the phone or drag to look, tap to select. Landscape works best.
  • VR headset: press the VR button, point a controller and pull the trigger.
  • AR: on a supported device, press the AR button to place the lab in your room.
